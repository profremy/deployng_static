Disclaimer

"Rewrite the Scroll" is a work of political art intended to provoke thought, inspire dialogue, and promote justice through Afrobeat music. It is not intended to incite violence or harm. The views expressed in the song reflect the artist's perspective and are meant to encourage peaceful activism and change.
